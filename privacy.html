Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

B&H Rides ("we," "our," or "us") operates as a community airport transportation service in Frisco, TX. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our ride booking service at bh-rides.netlify.app.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a ride request, we collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Home address
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Pickup location and airport destination
  • Ride date and time
  • Number of passengers
  • Preferred payment method
  • Any special notes or requests you provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To confirm and fulfill your ride request
  • To send you SMS and/or email notifications about your booking status
  • To contact you regarding your scheduled ride
  • To maintain records of completed rides for our internal business operations

We do not use your information for marketing purposes, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising.

3. SMS Notifications

By submitting a ride request and agreeing to our Terms of Service, you consent to receive SMS text messages from B&H Rides regarding your booking. These messages may include:

  • Ride confirmation or cancellation notices
  • Reminders about your upcoming ride
  • Updates from your driver

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us.

4. How We Store Your Information

Your ride request information is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database service with industry-standard encryption. We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill your ride and maintain our business records.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except as necessary to operate our service, including:

  • Twilio — used to send SMS notifications to you and our drivers
  • Formspree — used to deliver booking notifications to our team via email
  • Supabase — used to securely store ride request data

Each of these service providers is bound by their own privacy policies and data protection standards.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
